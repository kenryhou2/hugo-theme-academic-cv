I'm currently a staff researcher at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute and Biorobotics Lab, focusing on robotic perception, control, and sensor fusion. Previously, I was an MS in Robotics at CMU under Dr. Howie Choset, where I worked on real-time state estimation for systems supporting aerospace and legged robotics applications.

Before CMU, I was an Analog Applications Engineer at Texas Instruments, developing and testing power electronics hardware. I enjoy combining my hardware and software experience to build robotic systems that work in the real world.
