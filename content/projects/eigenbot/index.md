EigenBot is a modular robotics project exploring robot limb behavior, force sensing, and neural-controller ideas. The migrated portfolio media includes force-sensing experiments and a full-limb EigenBot platform image.

![EigenBot full limb platform](eigenbot_full_limb.JPG)

This work sits at the intersection of modular hardware, sensing, and controller development. See the [EigenBot project website](https://eigenbot-dnlc.github.io/) and watch the [EigenBot video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O39SGxDwapY&t=1s).

**Keywords:** EigenBot, modular robotics, force sensing, robot limbs, neural control, embedded sensing, physical robot experiments.
