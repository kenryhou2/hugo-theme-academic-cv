Medusa Space Arms is a robotics research project centered on arm motion and manipulation in a space-robotics testbed. The migrated site media shows carriage-mounted arm motion used to evaluate coordinated movement and sensing workflows.

The project connects mechanical system design, embedded sensing, and control implementation for a physical robot platform. Watch the [Medusa Space Arms video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Ck2ZWJHlsQ).
